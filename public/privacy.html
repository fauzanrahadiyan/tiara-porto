Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What we collect

When a client places an order we collect the name, WhatsApp number, brief details, and uploaded reference files/payment proof, used only to process the order.

TikTok and Instagram data

The admin tool connects only to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It stores access tokens securely and uses them only to publish the owner's videos and read basic account info. No data from other TikTok users is collected, sold, or shared.

Storage

Data is stored on Vercel and Upstash servers. Videos are deleted after publishing.

Threads and Instagram public content

The admin tools (Radar Tiara and Collab) use the Threads keyword search and Instagram public content features only to find public posts related to the owner's own business, so the owner can read and reply to them manually. We only read public posts and basic public profile info (username, post text, link, time). We do not collect private messages, we do not sell or share this data, and we keep it only as long as needed to show it in the admin dashboard, and then delete it.

Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you (order data, or any public post or profile info shown in our admin tools). Send an email to fauzan.rahadiyan@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your name, WhatsApp number, or social media username. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. If you logged in with Facebook, Instagram, or Threads, you can also remove the app's access from your account settings (Apps and Websites), and we will delete the related data.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your order data by contacting us. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time from the admin page or from TikTok settings.
