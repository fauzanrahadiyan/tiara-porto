Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What we collect

When a client places an order we collect the name, WhatsApp number, brief details, and uploaded reference files/payment proof, used only to process the order.

TikTok and Instagram data

The admin tool connects only to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It stores access tokens securely and uses them only to publish the owner's videos and read basic account info. No data from other TikTok users is collected, sold, or shared.

Storage

Data is stored on Vercel and Upstash servers. Videos are deleted after publishing.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your order data by contacting us. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time from the admin page or from TikTok settings.
